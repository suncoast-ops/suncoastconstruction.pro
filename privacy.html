Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Suncoast Construction Inc. ("Suncoast Construction," "we," "us," or "our") provides property maintenance, repair, and emergency services across Florida, Texas, and Georgia. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from customers and prospective customers, how we use it, and the choices you have — including how we handle mobile phone numbers and text message consent.

Information we collect

We collect only the information we need to quote, schedule, and complete work on your property:

  • Contact information — your name, mobile or landline phone number, email address, and mailing address.
  • Property and service information — the address where work is performed, the service you requested, access instructions, job notes, photos of the work area, and service history.
  • Communications — the content of calls, emails, and text messages you exchange with us, including quote requests and scheduling messages.
  • Billing information — invoice records and payment status. Card and bank details are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full payment card numbers.
  • Website information — basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed, collected by our web host for security and performance.

We collect this information directly from you when you call us, email us, sign a service agreement or work order, or contact us through our website. We do not purchase contact lists, and we do not buy phone numbers from third parties.

How we use your information

  • To prepare estimates and respond to quote requests.
  • To schedule, dispatch, and complete maintenance, repair, and emergency service.
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, technician arrival notices, and job status updates.
  • To invoice you and process payment.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To keep records required for warranty, licensing, insurance, and tax purposes.

Text messaging (SMS) and your consent

If you provide your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, we may send you service-related messages such as appointment confirmations and reminders, technician dispatch and arrival notifications, quote follow-ups, job completion updates, and replies to your questions.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or text message opt-in information and consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category of information sharing described in this Privacy Policy. This information will not be shared with any third parties for marketing purposes under any circumstance.

How consent is obtained

We obtain your permission to text you when you verbally provide your mobile number and agree to text updates during a phone call or in-person visit with our team, when you provide your mobile number and check the text messaging consent box on a written service agreement or work order, or when you text us first at our business number.

Message frequency, rates, and opt-out

Message frequency varies based on your active service requests. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us, and you can reply HELP or call (407) 915-9027 for assistance. Opting out of text messages does not affect our ability to serve you — we will simply contact you by phone or email instead. Full messaging terms are in our Terms and Conditions.

When we share information

We share information only in these limited situations, and never for a third party's marketing or promotional purposes:

  • Service providers. Vendors that operate our business on our behalf — such as our scheduling software, messaging platform, payment processor, and email provider. They may use your information only to perform services for us and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Technicians and subcontractors. The crew assigned to your job receives the property address, scope of work, and contact details needed to complete the service.
  • Property owners or managers. If work was authorized by a property manager, owner, or management company, we may share job status and completion records with that authorizing party.
  • Legal requirements. When required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfer. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information. Mobile phone numbers, text messaging opt-in data, and consent records are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and are excluded from all of the sharing described above.

Data retention and security

We keep customer and job records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, warranty, insurance, and tax obligations, then dispose of them securely. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. We may need to keep certain records where the law requires it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws; we honor those requests without discriminating against you for making them.

Children's privacy

Our services are directed to property owners and managers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes to how we handle text messaging will be posted on this page.
